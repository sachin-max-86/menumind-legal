Privacy Policy

MenuMind · Last updated: October 23, 2026

MenuMind ("the app," "we," "our") is an iOS app by Sachin Kumaran that digitizes restaurant menus and receipts using artificial intelligence, and helps you build orders and split bills. This policy explains what data the app processes, where it goes, and your choices. The short version: MenuMind has no user accounts, does not collect your name, email, contacts, or location, and does not sell or share your personal information.

1. Data we process, and why

DataPurposeWhere it goes
Photos of menus and receipts you choose to scan To digitize them into text (items, prices, categories) Sent over an encrypted connection through our server (AWS) to third-party AI model providers via OpenRouter (e.g., Google, OpenAI, and other model providers) for processing. We do not store your photos on our servers; they remain saved locally on your device.
Your dietary preferences and allergen list (if you set them) To flag menu items that may contain your allergens and to personalize suggestions Stored on your device. When you use allergen detection or recommendations, menu item names together with your allergen/preference list are sent to the AI providers above solely to generate the result.
Menu item details for dishes you look up with the ⓘ ("What is this dish?") button To show a short explanation of the dish and a representative photo The item's name, menu description, menu section, and menu language are sent through our server to the AI providers above to write the explanation. Our server then looks up a free-to-use photo from Wikipedia/Wikimedia Commons or, if none is found, from free stock photo libraries via Pexafy (such as Pexels, Unsplash, and Pixabay); only a short search phrase describing the dish is sent for this. Explanations and photo links are kept in a shared cache on our server (AWS, United States) so the same dish can be shown to other users without repeating the work. This cache holds dish information only and is not linked to your device or to you. When the card is shown, your device loads the photo directly from the site that hosts it, which, like any website, can see your device's IP address.
An anonymous device identifier (Apple App Attest key ID) To verify requests come from a genuine copy of the app and to enforce fair-usage limits (e.g., monthly scan quotas) Stored with monthly usage counters in our AWS database (United States). Counters are automatically deleted after roughly 60 days. This identifier is not linked to your name or any account.
Analytics and diagnostics To understand feature usage, fix crashes, and improve performance Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance): event counts (e.g., "menu scanned"), device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, and subscription tier (free/pro). No advertising identifiers and no precise location.
Purchases MenuMind Pro subscriptions Handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

2. What stays on your device

Your saved restaurants, menus, menu photos, dish explanations you have opened, orders, split-bill history, ratings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only. Deleting the app deletes this data.

3. What we don't do

  • No user accounts, and no collection of your name, email address, contacts, or photos other than the ones you explicitly scan
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising, no ad trackers
  • No sale or sharing of personal information with third parties for their own use

4. Third-party services

The app relies on the following processors, each receiving only the data described in section 1: Amazon Web Services (server infrastructure, United States), OpenRouter and its underlying AI model providers (menu/receipt/allergen processing and dish explanations), the Wikimedia Foundation and Pexafy (dish photo search, receiving only dish names or short search phrases from our server; photos are served by Wikimedia or by the original stock library, such as Pexels, Unsplash, or Pixabay), Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting), and Apple (payments, app distribution). Their handling of data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data retention

  • Photos and scan results: on your device until you delete them or the app
  • Server-side usage counters: automatically expire after ~60 days
  • Shared dish explanation cache (dish information only, not linked to any device): automatically expires after ~180 days; photo links from stock libraries are refreshed at least every 24 hours
  • Firebase analytics and crash data: retained per Google Firebase's standard retention periods

6. Your rights

Because MenuMind is account-less, most of your data is under your direct control on your device. You may contact us at any time to ask what data we hold, or to request deletion of server-side data associated with your device.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, correct, and delete personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Canadian residents (PIPEDA): you have the right to access and correct personal information we hold, and to withdraw consent by discontinuing use of the app and requesting deletion.

To exercise any of these rights, email onlysachinmax@gmail.com.

7. International transfers

Data described above is processed in the United States (and wherever the listed providers operate). By using the app you understand your data may be processed outside your country of residence.

8. Children

MenuMind is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. A note on allergen information and dish explanations

Allergen detection is generated by AI from menu text and is provided for convenience only. It may be incomplete or wrong. It is not medical advice — always confirm ingredients directly with the restaurant.

Dish explanations describe how a dish is typically made, are written by AI, and are not allergen information. Dish photos are representative only and may not match what the restaurant serves.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
